Paper Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Paper Reader is an iOS app that turns PDFs — research papers, slide decks, articles — into narrated audio. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data. The short version: the developer of Paper Reader collects nothing.

What the app stores, and where

  • PDFs you import, extracted text, and generated audio are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to any server run by the developer.
  • Your Gemini API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. It is never transmitted anywhere except directly to Google's API to authenticate your own requests.
  • App settings (narration voice, playback speed) are stored on device.

What is sent to Google

Paper Reader has no backend. To clean up a paper and narrate it, the app sends text extracted from the PDFs you import directly from your device to Google's Gemini API, authenticated with the API key you supply. Those requests are governed by Google's terms and privacy policy, and any usage is billed to your own Google account:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

Do not import documents you are not permitted to send to a third-party API.

What is not collected

Paper Reader contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting SDK, and no user accounts. The developer receives no data about you or your use of the app, and nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete a paper in the app to remove its PDF, text, and audio from your device. Settings › Remove API Key deletes your key from the Keychain. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. To delete data held by Google, use your Google AI Studio and Google account controls.

Children

Paper Reader is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
