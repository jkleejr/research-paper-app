Paper Reader — Support

Listen to research papers instead of reading them.

Paper Reader takes a research paper PDF, cleans up the text (dropping page furniture, citation clutter, and table debris), and narrates it with Google's Gemini text-to-speech so you can listen with the screen off.

Getting started

  1. Get a Gemini API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.
  2. Paste it into Paper Reader when prompted, or in Settings › Add API Key.
  3. Tap + and pick a PDF from Files or iCloud Drive.

Why do I need my own API key?

Paper Reader has no server and no subscription. Every request goes straight from your phone to Google using your key, so your usage is billed to your own Google account and your papers are never routed through anyone else's infrastructure.

What does it cost?

Text cleanup runs on Gemini's free tier for most papers. Narration uses Gemini's text-to-speech model, which requires billing enabled on your Google account — expect roughly $1–3 of Google usage for a typical full-length paper. Paper Reader itself takes no cut and charges nothing beyond the App Store price.

Troubleshooting

"That key didn't work"
Check the key was pasted whole with no trailing spaces, and that the Google project it belongs to has the Generative Language API enabled.
A paper fails partway through
Swipe right on the paper and tap Retry. Work already completed is kept, so a retry resumes rather than starting over and re-spending tokens.
Audio stops generating
Narration requires billing enabled on your Google account. Text cleanup works on the free tier, but text-to-speech does not.
Scanned PDFs come out empty
Paper Reader reads embedded PDF text and does not run OCR, so image-only scans cannot be narrated.
